Friction rules

  • Vehicle first: size and price appear the moment the model is picked, so nothing is a surprise later.
  • One decision per screen, sticky live total and deposit always visible, Continue button always in the thumb zone.
  • Resume anywhere (state saved on the device). Back never loses a choice.
  • Only name, phone, email and address are asked. Email is needed because Square sends the booking confirmation to the customer.
  • Pay button carries the amount. After Square checkout the Detail Pass appears only when the payment is confirmed.
  • Out-of-flow exits: text us, save my build, see recent work.
