Three audiences, three formats each. Rebuild any time with `npm run ads` (from the `site/` folder); prices and the phone
number come from the website's own data, so the ads follow the site.

| Used car lots | `used-car-lots-email-banner.jpg` | `used-car-lots-flyer.pdf` / `.png` | `used-car-lots-square-post.png` |

`overview.png` shows all nine at a glance.

## Using the email banners
- Put the banner at the very top of the email and link it to the audience link below. **Always keep real text under the
  image** (the emails in the chat): many inboxes block images, and an image-only email is more likely to land in spam.
- Alt text (paste into the image's "alt" field):
  - Property managers: "Mirror Finish Mobile Detailing: a resident perk that costs your property nothing."
  - Used car lots: "Mirror Finish Mobile Detailing: front-line ready, right where they sit."
  - Rideshare drivers: "Mirror Finish Mobile Detailing: your car is your paycheck, keep it spotless."
- Audience links (the tag after `?` lets you tell the channels apart once analytics is installed; the site works without it):
  - Property managers: `https://mirrorfinishmgm.com/?utm_source=property-managers&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=outreach`
  - Used car lots: `https://mirrorfinishmgm.com/?utm_source=used-car-lots&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=outreach`
  - Rideshare drivers: `https://mirrorfinishmgm.com/?utm_source=rideshare-drivers&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=outreach`

## Using the flyers
- Print the PDF on Letter paper (colour). The QR code opens the website with the audience tag (`utm_medium=flyer`). Tested:
  all three scan from the PDF.
- Attach the PDF to a follow-up email, hand it over in person, or leave it at the property or lot.
- The PNG version is for texting or posting.

## Using the square posts
Facebook and Nextdoor posts, group posts (read the group's rules first), and Instagram. Add the phone number and link in
the caption.

state it. Add such a claim only if it is true.
